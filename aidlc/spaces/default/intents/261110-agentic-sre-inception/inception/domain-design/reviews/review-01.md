### Summary

The decomposition is sound. The catalogue is well-formed and acyclic, ownership is single, and the ADRs record real alternatives. Two gaps deserve a decision before approval. First, no mechanism lets AgentSession observe the tool calls and denials that stateless agents make directly at the Gateway. Second, GuardrailCiGate does not say which copy of the policy file supplies the protected-path list, which affects the core "guardrails cannot be weakened by the PR" control. The remaining findings are tidy-ups that Contract Design or Functional Design can absorb.
