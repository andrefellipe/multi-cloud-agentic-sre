### Summary

The document is well structured. Every REQ has an EARS criterion, a source and a verification. The requirement IDs in traceability.json match requirements.md exactly, and the Q&A, assumptions, out-of-scope items and risks are handled with care. The three safety asks are mostly covered: protected paths (REQ-001 to 009), MCP allowlisting (REQ-003 to 004), and read-only AWS credentials (REQ-012 to 014). The main gap is that the guardrails can still be bypassed: the label gate has no stated owner-only control, the local and user settings layers are unprotected, and REQ-002 has no testable bound. The event-source authentication and "unsafe change" gaps should also be resolved before engineering starts.
