### Summary

The contract set covers every boundary in the DAG, and its cross-references resolve. Six Major gaps in the safety-critical write path remain, so a developer cannot implement the PR, webhook and lock behaviour without guessing: write idempotency, the `writes_enabled` and kill-switch ordering, the C2 failure path, the revert-PR merge loop, protection of the allowed-paths source, and the ledger race and lock lease. This is advisory, so the human can accept these risks or send the contracts back at the gate.
